## 🚀 HOW TO LINK YOUR WEBSITE TO GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE

### Step 1: Add Website URL to Google Business Profile

1. **Open Google Business Profile Manager**
   - Go to: https://business.google.com
   - Sign in with the Google account that manages your business

2. **Edit Your Business Info**
   - Click on your business "Ramya Tiles work contractor"
   - Click "Edit profile" or the pencil icon

3. **Add Website**
   - Find the "Website" field
   - After your site is live, add:

**Method 2: QR Code**
1. Google Business Profile → Get more reviews
2. Generate QR code
